Agent Hotwire

Agent Hotwire is an independent news and analysis channel about the video game Grand Theft Auto VI. We publish short videos and longer explainers on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

The videos are produced with the help of automated software (the “Agent Hotwire application”). The application researches publicly available information, drafts scripts, produces videos with a synthetic voice, and publishes them only to our own channels after automated quality checks.

What this application is, and is not

  • It is a private tool operated by one person for our own accounts.
  • It does not offer a service to other users, does not ask other people to sign in, and does not collect data about visitors of this site.
  • We are not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto is a trademark of its owner.
